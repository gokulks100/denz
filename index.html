Clinical Excellence Standards

The 4 Pillars of Dentz Safety & Tech

We invest in hospital-grade technology to guarantee your comfort, zero cross-contamination, and exceptional treatment accuracy.

01

Class-B Vacuum Autoclave

Strict European Class-B multi-cycle vacuum sterilization. All dental instruments are vacuum-sealed in sterile pouches and unsealed directly in front of the patient.

02

Rotary Endodontic Precision

Computerized micro-rotary motors and apex locators that navigate root canals with sub-millimeter precision, achieving painless 1-sitting root canals.

03

Low-Radiation Digital X-Ray

Instant digital intraoral radiography providing crisp high-resolution diagnostic imaging with up to 90% less radiation exposure than conventional film.

04

Zero-Tears Pediatric Protocol

Child-friendly psychology-guided treatment suite created by Chief Pedodontist Dr. Ranju MB to completely dissolve children's dental fear and anxiety.
